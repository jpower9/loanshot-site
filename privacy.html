LoanShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

LoanShot ("we", "the app") is a tool for mortgage loan officers to create and share branded loan scenarios. This policy explains what we collect and why. We keep it minimal. Everything below covers the LoanShot iPhone app, except the Website section, which covers this website only.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier. The app signs you in anonymously so your data can be saved and synced. No name, email, or password is required to use it.
  • Your brand profile — the information you choose to add: name, NMLS number, company, phone, photo, logo, and accent color. This is used only to brand the PDFs you create.
  • Scenarios you build — the loan figures you enter (amounts, rates, terms) and any saved borrower labels. These are numbers you type; the app does not import borrower credit, income, or personal financial records.
  • Basic usage counts — e.g. how many documents you've sent this month, to enforce free-tier limits.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your borrowers' personal information.
  • The app does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with Meta or other ad networks.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Where your data is stored

Brand profile and saved scenarios are stored on Supabase (our backend provider) and cached on your device. Access is restricted to your own account via row-level security.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (in-app purchase) or by Stripe (web checkout). We never receive or store your full payment card details; those are handled by Apple and Stripe under their own privacy policies.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it or request deletion. To delete your data, email us at the address below and we will remove your brand profile and saved scenarios.

Children

LoanShot is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Website (loanshot-site)

This section applies only to this website, not to the LoanShot app.

  • Meta Pixel. This website may use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc., to measure the results of our Facebook and Instagram ads. When you visit, the Pixel may set cookies and send Meta information such as the page you viewed, your browser and device type, your IP address, the ad campaign that brought you here, and whether you tapped the App Store button. Meta may link this to your Meta account and use it under Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • App Store link. The App Store button includes a campaign code so Apple can tell us, in aggregate, which ad led to a download. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Your choices. You can block the Pixel with your browser's tracking or cookie settings or a content blocker; the website works the same without it. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings (Accounts Center → Ad preferences).
  • Nothing you do on this website is linked to your LoanShot app account, and the app itself does not send data to Meta.
