Common questions

How do I brand my documents? Tap the profile icon → Brand Profile → add your name, NMLS #, company, phone, photo, and logo. Everything you send carries your branding.

How do the free limits work? The free tier includes 3 sends per month with a small watermark. Pro unlocks unlimited sends, full branding, and saved borrower profiles.

Are these numbers official? No. LoanShot produces estimates only — not a Loan Estimate or a commitment to lend. Every document includes that disclosure and your NMLS #.
