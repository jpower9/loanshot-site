LoanShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

LoanShot ("we", "the app") is a tool for mortgage loan officers to create and share branded loan scenarios. This policy explains what we collect and why. We keep it minimal.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier. The app signs you in anonymously so your data can be saved and synced. No name, email, or password is required to use it.
  • Your brand profile — the information you choose to add: name, NMLS number, company, phone, photo, logo, and accent color. This is used only to brand the PDFs you create.
  • Scenarios you build — the loan figures you enter (amounts, rates, terms) and any saved borrower labels. These are numbers you type; the app does not import borrower credit, income, or personal financial records.
  • Basic usage counts — e.g. how many documents you've sent this month, to enforce free-tier limits.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your borrowers' personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Where your data is stored

Brand profile and saved scenarios are stored on Supabase (our backend provider) and cached on your device. Access is restricted to your own account via row-level security.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (in-app purchase) or by Stripe (web checkout). We never receive or store your full payment card details; those are handled by Apple and Stripe under their own privacy policies.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it or request deletion. To delete your data, email us at the address below and we will remove your brand profile and saved scenarios.

Children

LoanShot is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
